One Page Tools

Small, self-contained browser tools. Each one is a single HTML file — nothing is uploaded, everything runs in your browser.

Nomination Document Builder →

Prepares a direction to transfer land to a nominee. Handles vendors, purchasers and nominees of any mix of types — individuals, companies, and trusts with corporate or individual trustees — then exports a formatted Word document or PDF with signing blocks.

Never Sold or Occupied Declaration →

Prepares the vendor's confirmation that a home is brand new and has never been leased, sold or occupied. Takes the property and title details and a vendor of any type — individual, company or trust — and exports a Word document or PDF with signing blocks.

Beneficial Owner Identification →

Records who stands behind a non-individual client, for customer due diligence. Takes a whole structure — a trust, its corporate trustee, a company or partnership above it — and produces a numbered form with the entity details and one register of the people behind it: directors, shareholders and beneficial owners, each entered once. Exports as a Word document or PDF.

Your data stays on your machine. These pages have no server and no database. Nothing you type is sent anywhere — documents are generated in the browser and saved straight to your computer. Drafts are saved as .json files wherever you choose.

To use a tool offline, save its page with Ctrl+S and open the saved file.
